      <span class="eyebrow">The Project</span>

      <h2>
        Protecting your digital footprint
        <span class="champagne-accent">while traveling.</span>
      </h2>

      <p>
        Stay Data Stay is a Python command-line application created to help
        travelers keep track of personal accounts they access on shared devices
        during hotel or vacation stays.
      </p>

      <p>
        Users enter information about their stay, including their hotel,
        location, room number, check-in and check-out dates, and the accounts
        they used. The application then organizes the information and provides
        reminders so users can remember to log out before leaving.
      </p>
    </div>

<section class="fade-in">

  <span class="eyebrow">How It Works</span>

  <h2>
    A simple reminder with a
    <span class="champagne-accent">security purpose.</span>
  </h2>

  <p>
    Users record the accounts they accessed during their stay, such as
    Netflix, YouTube, Prime Video, Google, or another service. Stay Data Stay
    creates a summary containing only the accounts selected by the user.
  </p>

  <p>
    The application uses check-out information to provide a final-day reminder,
    with the option for an earlier reminder, helping users remember an easy-to-miss
    digital privacy step before leaving their accommodations.
  </p>

</section>


<section class="fade-in">

  <span class="eyebrow">What I Learned</span>

  <h2>
    Turning an everyday problem into
    <span class="champagne-accent">application logic.</span>
  </h2>

  <p>
    Building Stay Data Stay helped me practice translating a real-world privacy
    concern into a structured application. I worked with user input, conditional
    logic, loops, validation, date information, account selection, and formatted
    output to create an experience that guides the user from beginning to end.
  </p>

  <p>
    The project also strengthened my understanding of designing technology
    around human behavior. A security solution does not always need to be
    complicated—the right reminder at the right time can help a user make a
    safer decision.
  </p>

</section>
