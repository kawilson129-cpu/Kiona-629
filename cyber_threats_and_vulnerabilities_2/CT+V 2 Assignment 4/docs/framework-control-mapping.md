# Framework and control mapping

## Applicability and status conventions

Aurum is assumed to be an OCC-supervised national bank. GLBA's customer-information safeguards are operationalized here through **12 CFR Part 30 Appendix B**; the FTC Safeguards Rule is not treated as this fictional bank's primary regulator rule. Actual data classification/customer-information scope and institutional legal applicability require organizational review. No actual customer NPI was used in testing. [15 USC 6801](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2022-title15/pdf/USCODE-2022-title15-chap94-subchapI-sec6801.pdf), [OCC interagency guidelines](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-I/part-30/appendix-Appendix%20B%20to%20Part%2030)

Mappings indicate **supporting controls**, not equivalence or satisfaction of every requirement. NIST CSF 2.0 is an outcomes framework; SP 800-53 Rev. 5 is a control catalog. This is a selected local crosswalk, not an official NIST crosswalk, baseline assessment, certification or statement of full compliance. SP 800-53 has a 2025 Release 5.2.0 update; the selected longstanding controls below remain identified by their Rev. 5 IDs. [CSF 2.0](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/CSWP/NIST.CSWP.29.pdf), [SP 800-53](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/53/r5/upd1/final)

Implementation and assurance are separate: **Implemented** = existing source contains the control; **Partially tested** = bounded local or historical evidence only; **Planned** = proposal not activated; **Unverified** = operating/deployed effectiveness not established. A control may be implemented and partially tested while production remains unverified.

No old CSF 1.1 identifier such as PR.DS-06 is presented as a CSF 2.0 outcome.

Fresh evidence is indexed in [control validation](control-validation.md); historical sources and authoritative links are in [evidence index](../evidence/index.md).
