Five safety/source-integrity checks also passed. Three dependency criteria failed; four enterprise acceptance areas were blocked. Overall **297 PASS / 3 FAIL / 4 BLOCKED**, with exact scope in [validation](control-validation.md). Missing operating evidence prevents a conclusion that Aurum's enterprise information-security program is effective.

Criteria: scoped control expectations in the [audit program](audit-program.md), the [framework crosswalk](framework-control-mapping.md) and stated dependency/release criteria. Procedures included source/document examination, metadata comparison and fresh isolated tests. No interviews, management representations, live access or remediation occurred. Review of historical evidence reconciled older failures rather than deleting them. Findings below distinguish observed conditions from unverified assurance gaps; severity follows the risk model and release gates, not test-count percentages.
