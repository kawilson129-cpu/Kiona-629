## 1. Purpose

This policy defines Aurum Capital Bank's internal requirements for daily operational reporting, workflow change notifications, interdepartmental handoffs, and the use of the current approved versions of internal procedures. It exists so that Operations staff and the departments that depend on them always work from the same, current instructions.

This is an internal Aurum Capital Bank policy describing bank rules and internal deadlines. The deadlines and procedures in this document are internal bank standards and are not statements of federal law.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all Operations staff and to every department that submits reports to, receives reports from, or hands work off to Operations. It covers daily reporting responsibilities, report submission deadlines, workflow change notifications, interdepartmental handoffs, updated procedural requirements, effective policy versions, conflicting procedure versions, and exceptions and escalation.

## 3. Policy Requirements

### 3.1 Daily reporting responsibilities
- Operations staff are responsible for producing the daily operations report each business day.
- The daily report must include transaction volumes, exceptions, pending items, and any processing delays.
- Report owners must verify figures before submission and correct errors promptly.
- Reports must be submitted through the approved reporting system or mailbox, not personal email.

### 3.2 Reporting submission deadlines (internal)
- **The daily operations report is due by 4:30 p.m. local time each business day.** (This deadline was updated effective 2026-10-01; the prior deadline was 5:00 p.m.)
- Weekly operations summaries are due by 12:00 p.m. on the first business day of the following week.
- End-of-month reports are due by 5:00 p.m. on the second business day after month-end close.
- If a deadline falls on a bank holiday, the report is due by the same time on the next business day.

### 3.3 Workflow change notifications
- Any change to a workflow, form, queue, or reporting requirement must be communicated to affected teams before it takes effect.
- Change notifications must state what is changing, the effective date, and who to contact with questions.
- No workflow change may take effect without a written notification from the responsible owner.
- Employees must acknowledge receipt of major workflow changes.

### 3.4 Interdepartmental handoffs
- A handoff between departments must include the work item, its status, the responsible owner, and any open issues.
- Handoffs must be confirmed in writing by the receiving team.
- Work must not be closed in one department until the receiving department confirms receipt.
- Handoffs involving client or confidential information must follow the applicable confidentiality policy.

### 3.5 Updated procedural requirements
- When a procedure is updated, the new version replaces the prior version as of its effective date.
- Employees must complete any required refresher or acknowledgment for updated procedures.
- Summaries of changes should accompany significant updates so employees understand what changed.

### 3.6 Effective policy versions
- Employees must always follow the **current approved version** of a procedure.
- Each procedure version carries a version number and effective date. The current version is the most recent approved version that has taken effect.
- Draft or superseded versions must not be used for live work.

### 3.7 Handling conflicting procedure versions
- When two versions appear to conflict, employees must use the version with the later effective date that is marked Approved.
- If it is unclear which version is current, employees must stop and confirm with the procedure owner before proceeding.
- Do not silently reconcile conflicting procedures; document the conflict and escalate it.
- The procedure owner is responsible for retiring or clearly marking superseded versions.

### 3.8 Exceptions and escalation
- One-off deviations from a reporting deadline or workflow must be approved in advance by the Operations manager.
- Systemic conflicts or repeated workflow issues must be escalated to the Operations Policy Owner.
- Any handoff dispute between departments must be escalated to the respective managers and, if unresolved, to the Operations Policy Owner.

## 4. Employee Procedures

### 4.1 To submit the daily operations report
1. Compile transaction volumes, exceptions, pending items, and delays.
2. Verify the figures and correct any errors.
3. Submit through the approved reporting system or mailbox by 4:30 p.m. local time.
4. Keep a copy of the submission for reference.

### 4.2 When a workflow changes
1. Read the change notification and note the effective date.
2. Acknowledge receipt if required.
3. Update your local checklists to match the new workflow.
4. Contact the listed owner with questions before the effective date.

### 4.3 To complete an interdepartmental handoff
1. Prepare the work item with status, owner, and open issues.
2. Send the handoff and request written confirmation.
3. Do not close your side until confirmation is received.
4. Retain the confirmation with the work item.

### 4.4 When procedure versions conflict
1. Compare the effective dates and approval status of each version.
2. Use the later-dated Approved version.
3. If it is still unclear, stop and contact the procedure owner.
4. Document the conflict and escalate if it is not resolved promptly.

## 6. Exceptions & Escalation

One-off exceptions to internal deadlines require advance approval from the Operations manager. Recurring or systemic problems, unresolved handoff disputes, and confusion about which procedure version is current must be escalated to the Operations Policy Owner (rachel.sampson@aurumbank.com). If a workflow issue creates a client or compliance risk, escalate to the relevant policy owner and to Compliance.

## 8. Frequently Asked Questions

**Has the daily operations reporting deadline changed?**
Yes. Effective 2026-10-01, the daily operations report is due by 4:30 p.m. local time each business day. The prior deadline was 5:00 p.m. Weekly summaries are due by 12:00 p.m. on the first business day of the following week.

**Which version of our interdepartmental handoff procedure should employees follow?**
Always follow the current approved version—the most recent version marked Approved that has taken effect. If two versions appear to conflict, use the one with the later effective date that is marked Approved, and if it is unclear, stop and confirm with the procedure owner.

**What should I do if I find conflicting procedure versions?**
Do not guess and do not silently reconcile them. Use the later-dated Approved version, document the conflict, and escalate to the procedure owner if it is not immediately clear which version is current.

**Who do I contact with workflow questions?**
The procedure owner listed in the change notification, or the Operations Policy Owner (Rachel Sampson) for systemic issues.

**Can I submit the daily report late?**
Only with advance approval from the Operations manager. Otherwise, submit by 4:30 p.m. local time.
