## 1. Purpose

This policy explains Aurum Capital Bank's internal rules for leave, time off, onboarding, and attendance, and how employees request leave and obtain HR guidance. It is written to give managers and employees a single reference for eligibility, approvals, and escalation so that leave decisions are handled consistently.

This is an internal Aurum Capital Bank policy describing bank rules and internal procedures. It is not legal advice and does not itself create or determine any legal leave entitlement. Where federal, state, or local law provides an entitlement (for example, family and medical leave), the law controls and the bank administers its obligations under that law. Employees with questions about a legal entitlement must contact Human Resources.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all Aurum Capital Bank employees, including full-time, part-time, and newly hired employees. It covers paid time off eligibility, parental and family leave guidance, leave request procedures, new employee onboarding, attendance responsibilities, required approvals, and HR clarification and escalation.

## 3. Policy Requirements

### 3.1 Paid time off (PTO) eligibility
- Full-time employees are eligible to accrue paid time off beginning on their first day of employment and may use accrued PTO after completing ninety (90) days of continuous service.
- Part-time employees accrue PTO on a prorated basis based on scheduled hours.
- PTO accrues per pay period up to a maximum accrual balance; accrual stops once the maximum is reached.
- PTO is determined by employment status, scheduled hours, and completed service—not by job title.
- PTO must be requested in advance and approved by the employee's manager before it is used, except where law requires otherwise.

### 3.2 Parental and family leave guidance
- Aurum Capital Bank provides parental leave for the birth, adoption, or placement of a child, and family leave for certain family and medical situations.
- **Internal bank rule:** eligible employees may receive up to sixteen (16) weeks of paid parental leave following the birth, adoption, or placement of a child, to be taken within twelve (12) months of the event.
- **Internal bank rule:** employees must provide as much advance notice as practical and must notify Human Resources as soon as the need for leave is known.
- Where a legal entitlement such as family and medical leave applies, Human Resources will administer the leave under that law, and the law controls. This policy does not reduce any legal entitlement.
- Employees should contact Human Resources to confirm eligibility and to complete the required forms.

### 3.3 Leave request procedures
- Submit leave requests through the bank's timekeeping or HR system, with dates and duration.
- Requests must be submitted at least ten (10) business days before the planned start of leave when the need is foreseeable.
- For unforeseeable leave, notify your manager and Human Resources as soon as possible, and no later than the first day of absence where practical.
- Human Resources will confirm whether the leave is paid, unpaid, or covered by a legal entitlement, and what documentation is required.

### 3.4 New employee onboarding
- New employees must complete onboarding within their first thirty (30) days, including benefits enrollment, policy acknowledgements, and required training.
- Managers must ensure new employees receive the tools, access, and introductions needed to begin work, coordinating system access through the IT Service Desk.
- Human Resources provides each new employee with information about leave, attendance, and how to ask policy questions.

### 3.5 Employee attendance responsibilities
- Employees are expected to report to work as scheduled and to notify their manager promptly when they will be absent or late.
- Unplanned absences must be reported before the start of the shift where possible.
- Employees must record time and leave accurately. Falsifying time records is a conduct violation.
- Repeated unplanned absences may be addressed under the bank's attendance and conduct processes.

### 3.6 Required approvals
- Leave requests require the employee's manager's approval.
- Requests lasting longer than ten (10) consecutive business days, and all parental or family leave, must also be reviewed by Human Resources.
- Human Resources must review any request involving a potential legal entitlement before it is approved or denied.

### 3.7 HR clarification and escalation
- Employees and managers should contact Human Resources when a leave situation is unclear, when a legal entitlement may apply, or when the policy seems ambiguous.
- If an employee believes a leave request was handled incorrectly, they may ask Human Resources to review the decision.
- Human Resources will document the question and the resolution, and will escalate to the Chief Human Resources Officer when needed.

## 4. Employee Procedures

### 4.1 To request paid time off
1. Confirm you have completed 90 days of service and have accrued PTO available.
2. Submit the request in the HR system at least ten business days in advance.
3. Obtain your manager's approval.
4. Record the time off accurately.

### 4.2 To request parental or family leave
1. Notify your manager and Human Resources as soon as the need is known.
2. Meet with Human Resources to confirm eligibility and any legal entitlement.
3. Complete the required forms and provide supporting documentation.
4. Work with your manager on coverage and a return-to-work plan.
5. Confirm in writing the start date, duration, and any pay arrangement.

### 4.3 If you are unexpectedly absent
1. Notify your manager as soon as possible, before your shift where practical.
2. Notify Human Resources if the absence may extend beyond a few days.
3. Provide documentation if requested.
4. Record the absence accurately when you return.

### 4.4 For a new employee
1. Complete onboarding within the first 30 days.
2. Complete benefits enrollment and policy acknowledgements.
3. Review leave, attendance, and how to ask policy questions.
4. Contact Human Resources if anything is unclear.

## 6. Exceptions & Escalation

Exceptions to internal leave procedures (for example, shorter advance notice) require Human Resources approval. Any situation involving a possible legal leave entitlement must be escalated to Human Resources, which will determine the bank's obligations under the applicable law. If an employee and manager disagree about a leave decision, escalate to Human Resources for review and, if needed, to the Chief Human Resources Officer.

## 8. Frequently Asked Questions

**How is paid time off eligibility determined?**
Eligibility is based on employment status, scheduled hours, and completed service—not job title. Full-time employees accrue PTO from their first day and may use accrued PTO after completing ninety (90) days of continuous service. Part-time employees accrue on a prorated basis.

**Where can I find parental leave guidance for a new employee?**
In Section 3.2 (Parental and Family Leave Guidance) of this policy. The bank's internal rule provides up to sixteen (16) weeks of paid parental leave within twelve months of a birth, adoption, or placement. New employees should contact Human Resources to confirm eligibility and complete the required forms; where a legal entitlement applies, Human Resources administers it under the law.

**How far in advance must I request leave?**
At least ten (10) business days before the planned start when the need is foreseeable. For unforeseeable leave, notify your manager and Human Resources as soon as possible.

**Who approves leave?**
Your manager approves leave requests. Requests longer than ten consecutive business days and all parental or family leave are also reviewed by Human Resources.

**Do I need approval before taking PTO?**
Yes. PTO must be requested in advance and approved by your manager, except where the law requires otherwise.
