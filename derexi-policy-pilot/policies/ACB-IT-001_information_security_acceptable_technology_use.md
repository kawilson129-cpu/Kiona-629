## 1. Purpose

Aurum Capital Bank relies on its technology, devices, and information systems to serve clients and to meet its regulatory obligations. This policy establishes the rules employees must follow to protect bank systems and confidential information from loss, theft, misuse, and unauthorized disclosure. It defines employee responsibilities for company devices, the required response when a device is lost or stolen, how security incidents are reported and escalated, which software employees may use, and how generative artificial intelligence tools may and may not be used.

This is an internal Aurum Capital Bank policy. It supports, but does not replace, federal requirements such as the GLBA Safeguards Rule; where a legal or regulatory requirement is stricter, the stricter requirement applies.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all Aurum Capital Bank employees, contractors, interns, and temporary staff, in every department, across all locations and remote work arrangements. It covers company-issued laptops and mobile devices, approved software, cloud services, generative artificial intelligence (AI) tools, and all bank confidential information. It applies whether an employee is working on bank premises, at home, or while traveling.

## 3. Policy Requirements

### 3.1 Employee responsibilities for protecting devices
- Employees are accountable for every company laptop, phone, tablet, and removable storage device assigned to them.
- Devices must be protected with full-disk encryption, a screen lock, and the bank's endpoint security tools at all times.
- Employees must never disable security software, share device passwords, or loan a company device to another person.
- Devices must not be left unattended in public places, vehicles, or unlocked offices. Lock the screen whenever you step away.
- Confidential or Restricted information must never be copied to personal devices, personal email, or personal cloud accounts.

### 3.2 Lost or stolen company laptops
- A lost or stolen company laptop, phone, or tablet is a **reportable security incident** and must be reported **immediately, and no later than one (1) hour after discovery**.
- Report the missing company laptop to the IT Service Desk at extension 4357 and by email to security@aurumbank.com. If a theft has occurred, also notify local law enforcement and obtain a report number.
- Provide the device asset tag, last known location and time, and whether the device was encrypted and screen-locked.
- Do not wait to see whether the device reappears. The Information Security team will remotely lock and wipe the device and will assess whether client or bank data may have been exposed.

### 3.3 Immediate incident reporting procedures
- Any suspected security incident must be reported immediately and no later than twenty-four (24) hours after discovery. Reportable events include phishing or social engineering, malware or ransomware, lost or stolen devices, account or credential compromise, and data loss or mishandling.
- Employees must not attempt to investigate, contain, or "fix" an incident on their own. Preserve the device, emails, and messages exactly as found and follow the directions of the Information Security team.
- Report incidents through the IT Service Desk, by email to security@aurumbank.com, or through the DeRexi assistant.

### 3.4 Approved versus unauthorized software
- Only software listed in the bank's **Approved Software Catalog** may be installed on company devices. The catalog is maintained by Information Technology.
- To request new software, submit a request to the IT Service Desk. Requests require manager approval and an Information Technology security review before installation.
- Installing unauthorized software, unlicensed software, browser plug-ins from untrusted sources, or personal applications on a company device is prohibited ("shadow IT").
- Downloading software from the internet onto a bank device without approval is grounds for access removal.

### 3.5 Acceptable use of generative artificial intelligence
- Employees may use generative AI only through tools that Information Technology has reviewed and approved for bank use, such as the bank's internal DeRexi assistant.
- **Employees must not upload, paste, type, or otherwise input internal bank documents, policy text, client or customer data, non-public financial information, source code, credentials, or any Confidential or Restricted information into public or external generative AI tools, including ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Microsoft Copilot (public), or similar public tools.**
- Approved AI tools may be used to help draft, summarize, or explain general, non-confidential material. Work product that informs a bank decision must still be reviewed by a qualified employee.
- Never rely on an AI tool as the final authority for a policy, regulatory, legal, or client decision.

### 3.6 Confidential information restrictions
- Information must be classified and handled according to the **Data Classification & Handling** policy. Confidential and Restricted information may be accessed only by employees with a legitimate business need.
- Confidential information may be transmitted only through bank-approved systems. Personal email, personal messaging apps, personal cloud storage, and unapproved AI tools are never approved channels.
- Suspected loss or improper disclosure of confidential information must be reported under Section 3.3.

## 4. Employee Procedures

### 4.1 If a company laptop is lost or stolen
1. Ensure your own safety first; do not confront a thief.
2. Report immediately (within one hour) to the IT Service Desk (extension 4357) and email security@aurumbank.com.
3. Provide the asset tag, the last known location and time, and whether the device was encrypted.
4. If stolen, file a police report and send the report number to Information Security.
5. Change the password for any account that was accessible from the device.
6. Cooperate fully with the remote lock-and-wipe and follow-up investigation.
7. Do not purchase a replacement device on your own; the IT Service Desk will issue one.

### 4.2 If you suspect any security incident
1. Stop work on the affected device or account.
2. Do not delete, forward, or alter anything related to the incident.
3. Report it immediately to the IT Service Desk or security@aurumbank.com.
4. Follow the triage and containment instructions you receive.
5. Document what you observed, when, and who else may be affected.

### 4.3 To request new software
1. Confirm the software is not already in the Approved Software Catalog.
2. Submit a request through the IT Service Desk with a business justification.
3. Obtain your manager's approval.
4. Allow Information Technology to complete a security review before installation.

### 4.4 To use generative AI safely
1. Confirm the tool appears on the approved AI tools list.
2. Use only general, non-confidential prompts.
3. Never paste internal documents, client data, or policy text into a public AI tool.
4. Have a qualified employee review any AI-generated output before it is used in bank work.

## 6. Exceptions & Escalation

Exceptions to this policy require written approval from the Chief Information Security Officer. A request must state the business need, the risk, and the proposed duration. Unresolved security incidents, suspected data exposure, and any request to use an unapproved AI tool must be escalated to Information Security (security@aurumbank.com). If an incident may involve client information, Information Security will notify Compliance.

## 8. Frequently Asked Questions

**What should I do if an employee reports a missing company laptop?**
Treat it as a security incident. Report it immediately to the IT Service Desk and security@aurumbank.com, within one hour of discovery. Provide the asset tag and last known location, change any accessible passwords, and let Information Security remotely lock and wipe the device. If it was stolen, file a police report.

**Can employees upload internal bank documents into ChatGPT?**
No. Employees must not upload, paste, or input internal bank documents, client data, or any Confidential or Restricted information into public or external generative AI tools such as ChatGPT. Only Information Technology-approved AI tools may be used, and only with non-confidential content.

**How quickly must I report a lost or stolen device?**
Within one hour of discovery. Security incidents generally must be reported no later than 24 hours after discovery.

**Can I install a free app to do my job faster?**
Only if it is in the Approved Software Catalog or approved through the IT Service Desk. Unapproved software on a bank device is prohibited.

**Who do I contact about a security concern?**
The IT Service Desk (extension 4357) or security@aurumbank.com. You can also ask the DeRexi assistant, but always report suspected incidents to Information Security.
