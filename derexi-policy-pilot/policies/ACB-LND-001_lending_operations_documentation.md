## 1. Purpose

This policy standardizes how Aurum Capital Bank accepts, verifies, documents, and processes loan applications, and how Lending staff handle incomplete applications and missing documentation. It protects the bank and its applicants by ensuring decisions are based on complete and verified information and by keeping applicant information confidential.

This is an internal Aurum Capital Bank policy. It supports the bank's obligations under Regulation B (ECOA) and Regulation Z (TILA) regarding applications, notices, and records; where a legal or regulatory requirement is stricter, the stricter requirement applies. Where this policy refers to an internal procedure or internal deadline, it is a bank rule and not itself a statement of federal law.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all Lending staff, including loan officers, credit analysts, processors, underwriters, and the Operations team that supports lending. It covers consumer and commercial loan application intake, document verification, incomplete and missing-document follow-up, exception approvals, handoffs to Operations, and the confidential handling of applicant information.

## 3. Policy Requirements

### 3.1 Loan application intake
- Every loan application must be logged in the loan origination system on the date it is received.
- Intake staff must record the applicant's name, the product applied for, the amount requested, the date received, and the channel through which it was received.
- The bank must record the application as complete or incomplete based on the required documents for that product.
- Applications must be time-stamped and assigned a unique application number for tracking.

### 3.2 Required document verification
- Required documents vary by product but typically include government-issued identification, proof of income, bank statements, and, where applicable, collateral or property documentation.
- Documents must be verified for authenticity, legibility, and consistency with the application before the file moves to underwriting.
- Any discrepancy must be documented and resolved with the applicant before a credit decision is made.

### 3.3 Handling incomplete credit applications
- An application is **incomplete** when the bank has received the application but the applicant has not provided all required information or documentation.
- Incomplete applications must not be advanced to underwriting or approval.
- Staff must document exactly which items are missing and issue a written request to the applicant listing the missing documents or information.
- The bank must retain a record of the incomplete-application notice and the applicant's response, in line with Regulation B recordkeeping expectations.

### 3.4 Missing documentation follow-up
- Follow up with the applicant promptly and in writing. A common internal standard is one follow-up at intake and a second follow-up within ten (10) business days if the applicant has not responded.
- If required documents are not received within the bank's stated window, the file may be closed as incomplete, and the applicant must be notified in writing.
- If the applicant later provides the missing documentation, the application may be reopened and re-dated as of the date the file becomes complete.
- All follow-up attempts must be documented in the loan file.

### 3.5 Loan processing restrictions
- No loan may be approved, committed, or disbursed while required documentation is missing or unverified.
- Staff may not substitute assumptions, verbal assurances, or informal notes for required documents.
- Processing staff may not alter, backdate, or remove documents from a loan file.
- A loan file that is incomplete must be clearly flagged as incomplete in the origination system.

### 3.6 Exceptions and approval procedures
- Exceptions to required documentation must be rare and documented. An exception request must state the missing item, the reason, the compensating controls (if any), and the risk.
- Routine exceptions for incomplete loan documentation must be approved in writing by the Lending Operations Manager.
- Exceptions that affect creditworthiness, fair lending, or a regulatory notice require approval by the Chief Lending Officer and notification to Compliance.
- No employee may approve an exception on their own application or for a related party.

### 3.7 Lending-to-Operations handoffs
- Lending must hand off a file to Operations only after all required documents are verified and the application is complete.
- The handoff must include the application number, the completed checklist, and any documented exceptions.
- Both teams must confirm the handoff in writing (email or system record) so accountability is clear.
- Operations must not proceed on a file that arrives without the completed checklist.

### 3.8 Confidential handling of applicant information
- Applicant information is Confidential and must be shared only with employees who have a business need.
- Applicant documents must be stored only in bank-approved systems, never on personal devices or personal email.
- Applicant information must not be discussed in public areas or with anyone outside the approval chain.

## 4. Employee Procedures

### 4.1 To process a new loan application
1. Log the application in the origination system on the date received and assign an application number.
2. Build the required-document checklist for the product.
3. Verify each document for authenticity, legibility, and consistency.
4. Mark the file complete or incomplete and document any missing items.
5. Advance only complete, verified files to underwriting.

### 4.2 When required documents are missing
1. Do not advance the application.
2. Document exactly which documents or information are missing.
3. Send the applicant a written request listing the missing items.
4. Follow up within the bank's stated window; document each attempt.
5. If the documents are not received in time, close the file as incomplete and notify the applicant in writing.
6. If the applicant later provides the documents, reopen and re-date the file when complete.

### 4.3 To request an exception for incomplete documentation
1. Confirm that closing the file or waiting is not the better option.
2. Draft an exception request naming the missing item, the reason, and the risk.
3. Obtain the required written approval based on Section 3.6.
4. Record the exception in the loan file and the origination system.
5. Notify Compliance if the exception touches credit, fair lending, or a required notice.

### 4.4 To hand off a file to Operations
1. Confirm the file is complete and all documents are verified.
2. Prepare the handoff record with the application number and checklist.
3. Send the handoff and obtain written confirmation from Operations.
4. Retain both records in the loan file.

## 6. Exceptions & Escalation

Documentation exceptions are handled under Section 3.6. Escalate to the Lending Operations Manager for routine exceptions, to the Chief Lending Officer for higher-risk exceptions, and to Compliance whenever an incomplete application involves a required Regulation B notice, a fair-lending concern, or a potential regulatory issue. If an applicant's information may have been mishandled, escalate to Information Security.

## 8. Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I process a loan application when required documents are missing?**
No. If required documents are missing, the application is incomplete and must not be advanced to underwriting, approved, or disbursed. Document the missing items, send the applicant a written request, and follow up. The file can be reopened if the documents arrive later.

**Who can approve an exception for incomplete loan documentation?**
Routine exceptions must be approved in writing by the Lending Operations Manager. Exceptions that affect creditworthiness, fair lending, or a regulatory notice require the Chief Lending Officer's approval and notification to Compliance. No employee may approve an exception on their own application.

**What happens if the applicant never provides the missing documents?**
The file may be closed as incomplete and the applicant must be notified in writing. If the documents are provided later, the application may be reopened and re-dated as of the date it becomes complete.

**What documents are required?**
It depends on the product, but typically government-issued identification, proof of income, bank statements, and collateral or property documents where applicable. Verify every document before advancing the file.

**How do I know I can hand off a file to Operations?**
Only after all required documents are verified and the application is complete. Include the completed checklist and any documented exceptions, and get written confirmation from Operations.
