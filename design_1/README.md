# Design 1 · Stay Data Stay

Five linked assignments explore a traveler's account-review experience at checkout.
The repository project is **Stay Data Stay** (called “State Data Stay” in the prompt).
Existing Design 1 program work and authentic FigJam ideation supply the strongest
cohesive evidence, so no DeRexi features or unrelated coursework are rebuilt.

## Research citations

Three distinct sources were opened and reviewed; findings, limitations and design
decisions are documented in A2:

1. [Netflix — How to use Netflix on your hotel TV](https://help.netflix.com/en/node/25814).
2. [Google Account Help — See devices with account access](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/3067630?hl=en).
3. [Australian Government Smartraveller — Cyber security when travelling overseas](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/while-overseas/protecting-yourself-crime/cyber-security-when-travelling-overseas).

The sources support relevant provider/security practices, not market demand,
forgetting rates or measured product effectiveness. A blocked CISA PDF is recorded
as excluded rather than counted as a verified fourth source.
