# DeRexi Policy Owner Guide

Use the [Employee guide](employee-guide.md) for common functions. Owners also see department policy health and working copies; this does not grant self-approval or organization-wide incident management.

## Dashboard and clarification

Open **Dashboard** to prioritize departmental reviews, policy status and feedback, plus authorized personal clarification/incident aggregates. These are operational indicators, not compliance certification. In **Messages**, answer assigned questions, stay within the published policy and resolve the thread when appropriate. Escalate exception requests to the responsible authority.

## Drafting and document intake

1. Open **Governance**. Start a draft, template, existing-policy revision or DOCX/PDF import.
2. Browse or drop a document below 5 MB. Extracted text must fit 20,000 characters. Image-only/scanned PDFs require a text-based source; OCR is not provided. Encrypted, malformed or unsafe documents may be rejected.
3. Review the extracted preview against the original. Correct title, summary, body, category, version, effective date and review date. Extraction can lose formatting.
4. Confirm you reviewed the content, then save. A preview is not a saved draft. Keep your working text until save succeeds. Original downloads require configured private retention; successful text intake does not guarantee original-file storage.
5. For a revision, confirm the selected published baseline and replacement text. The current Library version remains published until the replacement is explicitly published.

## Independent review

1. In **My Drafts**, submit to another **Policy Owner in the same department**. If no eligible peer exists, ask Admin; do not create users or change roles.
2. The assigned peer opens **Peer Reviews**, checks content/history and approves, requests revisions or rejects. Revision/rejection decisions require explanatory comments.
3. Peer approval sends the item to an independent System Administrator for final approval. The author and peer cannot provide final approval.
4. Address comments, save and resubmit. **Every saved edit clears the reviewer and both approvals**; fresh review is required.
5. After final approval, Admin explicitly publishes on or after the effective date. There is no automatic publication scheduler. Check the published Library version afterward.

States: Draft → Pending peer review → Pending final approval → Approved pending publication → Published. Revisions requested or Rejected returns work to the author. Published working copies cannot be edited; create a new revision. Review History records decisions and comments.

## Pulse

Open **Pulse**, filter relevant publications and verify the original publisher, date and status. A proposed rule is not a final rule. Use the policy action to create a source-linked draft/revision, confirm the policy baseline and follow the same independent workflow. Pulse never automatically approves or publishes policy. Stale/unavailable-source notices limit freshness; verify the original publication before acting.

## Errors and escalation

- **409 / draft changed:** preserve text, refresh and compare. A changed published baseline requires a fresh revision.
- **Review invalidated:** renew peer/final review after edits or role changes; do not bypass it.
- **Preview expired:** upload again and confirm. Finish/discard other previews if capacity is reached.
- **503 / workspace unavailable:** retry later and notify Admin; missing deployment setup is not fixed by repeated saves.
- **403/404:** check department/assignment and ask Admin. Owners cannot grant access or inspect another department's working copies.
- **Future effective date / duplicate version:** correct the version or wait until publication is eligible.

Urgent security concerns use incident reporting and the normal security contact route, not the policy-review queue.
