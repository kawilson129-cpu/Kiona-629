# DeRexi Employee Guide

Sign in with your institution-authorized account and check **Signed in as**. Production users cannot switch to another person's identity.

## Policy Assistant and Library

1. Open **Policy Assistant** and ask one clear question, such as “How long do we keep loan application records?” Avoid customer account numbers, credentials and unnecessary personal information.
2. Read the answer and its **Policy Reference**. Check the cited title, version and scope before acting.
3. If the answer needs clarification, open its linked **Messages** thread. Explain what is missing and send your question to the assigned policy owner. A routed question is not an approved policy exception.
4. Open **Library** to browse/search published policy directly. Working drafts are not employee guidance.

Search matches wording rather than every possible meaning. Rephrase a broad question or contact the owner when the reference does not fit.

## Messages and feedback

Open **Messages** or the notification bell, select your thread, read the owner's reply and reply in the same conversation. Notifications indicate updates, not necessarily resolution. You can read authorized conversations, not every employee's inbox. For unresolved or misrouted questions, contact the owner or ask Admin to review routing.

On a DeRexi answer, select **Yes** or **No** to “Was this helpful?” Wait for saved confirmation. A failed save retains the previous saved choice; retry after connectivity returns. Feedback does not replace incident reporting or urgent contact.

## Report a suspected incident

1. Open incident reporting in the Assistant.
2. Describe what happened without secrets. Answer the incident-type follow-ups, then review/edit your details. Your verified identity and department determine the approved IT/Security route; you cannot enter an arbitrary recipient.
3. Review the structured request and explicitly confirm before submission. Confirmation creates an internal reference; it does not itself send email. Check the handoff status and attempt delivery only through the approved workflow. Outbound delivery may be disabled.
4. SMTP acceptance is not proof of delivery or ticket creation. Use the institution's existing IT ticketing/reporting channel for investigation and resolution. An administrator can review the internal reference; employees cannot assign handlers or edit incident-management fields.
5. Retry only when the interface says the failure is safely retryable and the waiting period has elapsed. Unknown or pending outcomes require IT/Security verification using the reference; do not submit a duplicate to force a result. Earlier internal reports remain available in your report history.

If approved routing is unavailable, expand **Record an internal reference without email**. Choose the category, review your details, tick the confirmation checkbox and submit. This preserves internal tracking only: no email is sent and no ticket creation is confirmed. Use the established IT/Security channel for urgent reporting and investigation.

For an active threat, follow your institution's urgent security/SOC contact procedure; do not wait for a message reply. Submission is not acknowledgement that a responder has begun work.

## Permissions and errors

Employees use Assistant, Library, authorized Messages, answer feedback, incident reporting and Pulse. Governance and policy-health Dashboard require an Owner/Admin role. Explicitly assigned reports may be visible to their handlers; only Admins manage them.

| State | Action |
|---|---|
| Session expired / sign-in required | Sign in again; contact Admin if the authorized account is refused. |
| Access denied / not found | Check your account and thread. Ask Admin; do not try another user's ID. |
| Empty Library/search | Clear filters, try a narrower term, then contact the policy owner. |
| Request failed | Preserve text, retry once when online and verify whether it saved. |
| No owner response | Follow departmental escalation or ask Admin to review routing. |

Sign out when finished, especially on shared devices.
